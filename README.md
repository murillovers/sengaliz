# Sengaliz: Style & Uniforms

Sengaliz is an online marketplace specializing in high-end apparel, tailored uniforms, and exclusive fashion designs.

The platform provides a curated selection of sophisticated garments suitable for formal events and professional settings. It integrates a comprehensive design system and brand identity to maintain a consistent aesthetic across its digital storefront. Users can browse unique collections that emphasize elegance and craftsmanship in both casual and formal wear.
